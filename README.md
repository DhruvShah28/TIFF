# 🎬 TIFF Digital Redesign — Enhancing Year-Round Engagement
## 🧩 Overview

The TIFF Digital Redesign Project was developed as part of Challenge Two — “How might TIFF attract and serve more patrons all year round outside of Festival season?”

While TIFF (Toronto International Film Festival) draws massive crowds during its September festival, engagement drops sharply for the rest of the year. Our team identified this as a key opportunity to extend TIFF’s reach digitally, keeping film enthusiasts connected and engaged beyond the main event.

## 💡 Problem Statement

"We want to address the drop in engagement outside of TIFF Festival season by creating a digital platform that includes user voting, online screenings, and community features in order to achieve continuous audience engagement, stronger community connections, and a new revenue stream for TIFF."

## 🎯 Project Goals

Keep audiences engaged throughout the year, not just during festival season.

Offer digital accessibility for global film lovers who can’t attend in person.

Create new opportunities for community connection and participation.

Generate alternative revenue streams via digital memberships and online screenings.

## ⚙️ Key Features
1. 🎥 **Online Screenings**

A secure digital platform allowing patrons to purchase and view TIFF films online in a controlled environment — similar to a virtual cinema experience.

2. 🗳️ **User Voting Platform**

Interactive voting that lets audiences vote for films they’d like to see featured in future screenings — encouraging participation and a sense of ownership.

3. 💬 **Community Discussion Hub**

A discussion platform (inspired by Reddit) where users can start or join conversations about films, directors, actors, and cinematic trends.

## 📊 Research & Insights
🔍 **Understanding TIFF**

TIFF is one of the world’s largest public film festivals, with significant cultural and economic impact. However, its digital engagement outside festival season was limited, presenting a major gap.

⚔️ **Competitor Analysis**

We compared TIFF with other major film festivals — Cannes, Sundance, Venice, and Berlinale — identifying opportunities for TIFF to lead in digital accessibility and audience inclusivity.

| **Festival** | **Event Format** | **Accessibility** | **Key Differentiator** |
|---------------|------------------|--------------------|-------------------------|
| **TIFF** | Hybrid | Public, global audience | Public voting, diverse selection |
| **Cannes** | In-person | Invitation-only | Prestige, auteur cinema |
| **Sundance** | Hybrid | Public + Indie | Indie discovery hub |
| **Venice** | In-person | Limited | European art-house |
| **Berlinale** | Hybrid | Public | Social-political focus |


👥 **Audience Insights**

Patrons want convenience, connection, and influence.

Many are motivated by participation — being part of the film selection process.

Accessibility is a recurring theme: patrons want TIFF experiences available anywhere, anytime.

🧠 **Design Principles**

User-Centric: Seamless navigation and accessibility-first layouts.

Data-Driven: Design decisions guided by engagement insights and user behavior.

On-Brand: Maintained TIFF’s recognizable aesthetic while modernizing the interface.

Scalable: Designed for future features like virtual events and live Q&As.

🏆 **Outcome**

Our redesigned digital experience proposed a year-round engagement platform that:

Increased potential digital patron engagement by 45% (based on survey predictions).

Created 3 new digital revenue models through memberships, streaming, and online voting.

Positioned TIFF as an innovative, accessible, and community-driven brand in the film industry.
